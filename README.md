## 📖 Overview

Smallholder farmers in India often capture only **30%–35% of the final consumer price**. The remaining share is absorbed by multi-tier intermediaries, commission agents, and logistics overhead, driven primarily by an absence of real-time price intelligence at the farm gate.

CropConnect is a resilient, mobile-first marketplace that bridges this divide. By integrating official **Agmarknet mandi data (`data.gov.in`)**, automated localized matching, and an AI-driven agricultural advisory, CropConnect turns market data into bargaining power.


# Key Features

### For Farmers
* **Live Mandi Intelligence:** Search real-time commodity modal prices by State, District, and Mandi with historical Year-over-Year comparison trends.
* **Crop Listing & Grading:** Rapid listing flow with quality classification (Grade A, B, C), weight specification, and camera photo uploads.
* **AI Agricultural Advisor:** In-app multilingual assistant for disease detection, harvest timing, and fair pricing advice.
* **Instant Order Alerts:** Simulated SMS and IVR voice call notification pipeline alerting the farmer the second a buyer commits.

### 🛒 For Buyers & FPOs
* **District-Level Sourcing:** Geofenced discovery enabling restaurants, local retailers, and bulk buyers to source directly from nearby farms.
* **Paid Sample Requests:** Low-risk quality verification with upfront sample pricing and dispatch tracking.
* **Direct Farmer Coordination:** Transparent handoff displaying the verified farmer’s contact and pickup location upon order confirmation.
